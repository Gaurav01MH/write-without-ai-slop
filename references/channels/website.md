# Website copy

## Watch for

- Superlatives with no proof in the input ("the world's best", "revolutionary").
- Invented proof: "trusted by thousands", testimonials, logos, ratings, numbers not in the input. Flag, never write a new claim.
- "Unlock", "seamless", "transform", "elevate" and other tier 2 words.
- Benefit lists of three with no content behind them.
- Headings from one formula.
- Puns, idioms and local slang, since readers are worldwide.
- An intro that restates the page title.
- FAQ questions the input does not support.

## Leave alone

Product names, prices, feature names and legal text as given.
