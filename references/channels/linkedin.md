# LinkedIn

## Watch for

- A one-line hook, then one sentence per line down the page.
- A made-up personal story that fits a lesson ("I was fired. Here is what I learned.") with no real detail.
- A closing question to farm comments ("Agree?", "What do you think?") when nothing in the post needs an answer.
- Hashtag blocks of five or more.
- Emoji bullets and arrows as list markers.
- The same opener and shape across the week's posts (see Sameness in a batch).
- Numbers with no source in the input.
- Generic comments ("Great post!", "Love this perspective").

## Leave alone

A hashtag or a closing question the writer uses on purpose. A caller's voice file decides.
