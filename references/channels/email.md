# Email

## Watch for

- An opening line about the sender ("I hope this email finds you well", "I wanted to reach out").
- Fake personalization: praise for "your recent work" with no named detail.
- A vague ask ("Let me know your thoughts") instead of one clear question.
- A subject line that does not say the purpose, or that hypes.
- Exclamation marks used for warmth.
- Polished text with no substance.
- Equal-length sentences and a formula structure.
- Stock sign-offs ("Looking forward to hearing from you").
- Any claim about the recipient or their company that is not in the input.

## Leave alone

Plain greetings the writer normally uses. A short email with one polite line is not a tell.
