# Slack and chat

## Watch for

- Headers, bold sections or bullets on a message of two or three lines. Formatting suits longer messages.
- A formal greeting and sign-off in a quick team message.
- Restating the question before answering.
- Closers such as "Hope this helps!" and "Let me know if you have any questions!"
- A wall of text where a thread, a reaction or a one-line answer would do.
- Several short messages in a row where one would do.
- An @channel or @here for a non-urgent item.
- Markdown or other formatting syntax in a place that may show it raw.
- Invented reasons, times or names. If the input only says the deploy moved to Thursday, write that and nothing more.

## Leave alone

Casual tone and the emoji the team already uses. Short messages are fine.
