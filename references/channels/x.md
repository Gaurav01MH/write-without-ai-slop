# X

## Watch for

- A reply that agrees and adds nothing ("Great point!", "This is so true.").
- A closing question on a reply or post to farm engagement.
- A thread opener that promises ("A thread on...") when post one does not carry the point.
- Repeating the same hook or text across posts in a batch.
- Hype with no number, date or source when the post is about a result. Generic hype is what people mute.
- Hashtag stacks and posts padded to fill the space.
- Every post in a thread with the same length and rhythm.

## Leave alone

Short and blunt is normal on X. Do not flag brevity or fragments alone. A human reply that is polite and polished is not a tell by itself.
