# Words and phrases

A word is never banned on its own. Flag it by tier. Sources are in [docs/sources.md](../docs/sources.md).

## Tier 1: always fix

Chatbot leftovers (see patterns, item 15). Examples: "Certainly!", "Great question", "I hope this helps", "As an AI language model", "as of my last update", "[Your Name]".

## Tier 2: fix when two or more appear in one paragraph

- delve, delves, underscore, underscores, showcase, showcasing, pivotal, crucial, additionally, notably, comprehensive, enhancing, insights.
- leverage, utilize, empower, foster, robust, facilitate, key (as an adjective), transform, deliver, deploy, drive, tackle, impact (as a verb), going forward, one-stop shop.
- seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, game-changing, transformative, elevate, unlock, harness, landscape, realm, tapestry, journey (when not about travel), vital.

## Tier 3: fix when repeated

- "it's worth noting", "it's important to remember", "when it comes to", "at the end of the day", "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "in order to".
- "please note", "at this time", "simply", "it's easy", "quickly" in instructions.

## Not on any list

Plain words that happen to appear in AI text, such as "important" or "help", are not tells. Do not flag a word only because a model might use it.
